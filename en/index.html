Signals, from Zero to Expert

A visual course in signal processing, from the Ferris wheel to synchrosqueezing

Learn signal processing with your eyes and your ears. You do not need to know anything beforehand: each idea starts with a moving picture, you test it with challenges you can touch and listen to, and the equations wait folded away in a separate corner for anyone who wants them.

Start with lesson 1 See the course map ↓

    A Ferris wheel turns at constant speed, and the height of its cabin, noted instant by instant, draws a wave: the whole course starts from that picture.

    How it works

    • Each lesson has four parts

      The idea in a moving picture, step-by-step challenges you solve by touching and listening, a lab called “In your voice” where you try it on your own recording, and a short quiz that explains every answer.

    • Your progress is saved in this browser

      The challenges you complete and your exam scores stay here, with no accounts or sign-ups. If you open the course on another computer or on your phone, it will start from zero.

    • There is a short route to synchrosqueezing

      If what you want is to get to synchrosqueezing, 14 lessons are enough: 1 to 5, 11 to 13, and 15 to 20. Each one stands on its own, so you can leave the others for later.
