Four seasons and a final

Exams

At the end of each season there is an exam with two questions from each lesson, and at the end of the course there is one with a single question from each lesson. The questions are drawn at random from the lessons’ question banks, each one explains why whether you get it right or not, and you pass with 70%. You can retake them whenever you like, and your best score is saved in this browser.
